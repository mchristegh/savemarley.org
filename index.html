From the Desk of the Candidate

A Letter to Missy

Dear Missy,

I know about the tabs. The Humane Society tabs. I saw you scrolling. I saw you smile at a dog who isn't me. I didn't say anything. I just lay on the floor and stared at the wall for a while.

Do you remember the walks? Every one of them, I was there. I sniffed every single thing for you. That's not nothing, Missy. That's a lot of sniffing.

I know I'm not perfect. There was the skunk. There are the crumbs. There is the face I make on Ears Day, which I'm told is "a lot." But I'm YOUR imperfect. Well, your shared imperfect.

Those dogs on the website deserve a wonderful home. I hope they all find one. Just maybe not one where I already live, and I'm already the dog, and everyone already loves me.

And if it turns out to be a golden retriever, Missy, I don't know what I'll do. You know how I feel about golden retrievers. Everyone knows. The neighbors know.

So please. Close the tabs. Grab a leash. Throw a ball. I'll bring it back, I promise (eventually).

Love forever,
Marley

Stinky Dog · Crumb Bum · Your Very Good Boy 🐾
